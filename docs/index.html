pfUI Octo for OctoWoW

Your whole interface. One addon. ✨

pfUI Octo is a free all-in-one interface for WoW 1.12 (OctoWoW), version 1.0.0, made by Bdot89. Unit frames, action bars, nameplates, damage and healing meters, threat, combat text, a swing timer, gear sets and whole UI layouts, all set up from one clean options window with a live preview on every page. 🎛️

🧩 Built on pfUI by Shagu, so everything that works with pfUI keeps working: pfQuest, Clickcast, your profiles and your settings. It just does a lot more, and looks better doing it.

🌟 What you get

  • 🗺️ UI layoutsNormal, Raid, PvP or your own: each keeps its own frame positions, meters, gear set and even action bar spells. Switch with one click, or let it switch by itself in raids, dungeons, battlegrounds and arenas
  • 🧍 Unit framesPlayer, target, focus, party and raid, with borders, fading health, combat flash, class icons, elite and rare marks, dispel colours and buff timers. Click any part of the preview to change it
  • ⚔️ Action barsBartender-style bars with 14 button looks, proc glows, cooldown swipes, hover and press effects, fade in and out of combat, and end-cap art
  • 🎯 NameplatesPlater-style plates: 8 ready looks, auras with timers, cast bars, threat colours, quest marks, class icons, range fading and your own "when this, do that" rules
  • 📊 Meters & threatDamage, healing, interrupts, dispels, deaths and more in up to 6 windows, with a full breakdown of every spell, target and death. Threat with a pull warning on the target's plate
  • 💥 Combat textScrolling combat text in five areas you place yourself, with looks, crits, sounds and alerts
  • ⏱️ Swing timerMain hand, off hand and ranged swings, parry haste, queued attacks, Aimed Shot and Multi-Shot clipping, Slam and Seal-twist marks
  • 🛡️ Gear setsItem Rack-style sets that swap at once, a trinket queue, slot buttons, automatic swaps when you mount, swim, stealth or enter a battleground, and talent specs at the Goblin Brainwashing Device
  • 🎒 Bags, chat, minimapBags with instant sorting, chat with timestamps and class colours, a SexyMap-style minimap with shapes, art and a clock, loot roll history
  • 🧭 Layout mode/moveui: drag frames, snap them to each other, to the grid or to mirrored guide lines, match sizes, lock frames together and hide what you don't need
  • 🔁 pfUI Octo or BlizzardEvery section has a switch at the top of its page: go back to Blizzard's frames for that part at once, no reload
  • ⚡ Faster, smootherCalmer nameplates in big pulls, unit frames that only listen to their own unit, and much more, so your frame rate stays up

Your settings are saved per character and stay through /reload and logging in. Share a whole setup as one text with /export and /import. 💾

🗺️ UI layouts

Your layouts as coloured tiles: click one to switch, right-click to rename it or give it a picture and colour. Each remembers where every frame sits, which meters show, a gear set and your own spells on the bars. Below: which layout to use in the open world, dungeons, raids, battlegrounds and arenas.

🧍 Unit frames

Pick a frame from the tabs (player, target, focus, party, raid...) and the preview shows your real frame. Every part is clickable: the health bar, the texts, the portrait, the buffs. Drag the texts to move them, Shift + wheel to resize.

⚔️ Action bars

Your real bars on a mini screen and a sample button that plays every effect: proc glow, cooldown, hover and press. Click a bar for its settings, Shift + wheel for its button size, and pick a look from 14 picture tiles.

🎯 Nameplates

Four sample plates: your target, one casting, a quest mob and a friendly player. Change anything and the preview follows. "Test on real nameplates" shows threat colours and casts on the plates around you.

📊 Meters

Up to six windows, each with its own look. "Test fight" fills them with a made-up Onyxia fight so you can style them without fighting, and "Open breakdown" shows the full Details!-style report.

💥 Combat text

A small copy of your screen with each text area where it really is. Drag an area to move it, click it for its settings, and watch sample hits, heals and crits float by.

⏱️ Swing timer

Main hand, off hand, ranged and cast bars in the bar and border style you pick, with the warrior's Slam window and the paladin's Seal-twist marks shown right on the preview.

🛡️ Gear sets

Your character between the slot columns, like the character window. Click a slot to pick an item from your bags, untick slots to leave them out, then Save. Wear a set with a click, a key, the minimap button or the set button.

💍 Trinket queue

List your trinkets in order for each slot: when the one you wear is on cooldown, the next ready one goes on by itself. Priority, pause and delay per trinket.

🧠 Talent specs

Give each talent spec at the Goblin Brainwashing Device a gear set, a UI layout and its own spells on your bars. Switch spec and everything follows.

🔄 Automatic swaps

Put on a set when you mount, take a flight path, swim, stealth, enter a battleground, raid or dungeon, rest or start a fight, and put your gear back after.

🎚️ Slot buttons

A bar with buttons for any equipment slot: click to use the item, hover to swap it for another from your bags. Bind them to keys in the Key Map.

💬 Chat

A preview of your chat with timestamps, class colours and short channel names, plus a separate Whispers tab and the Loot & Spam window.

🧭 Minimap

SexyMap-style shapes, borders and spinning art, a clock, and every addon button: put it on the panel, on the map, or hide it.

🎒 Bags

One bag window with your columns, slot size and spacing, and one-click sorting (instant with ClassicAPI).

⚡ Runs even better with

pfUI Octo works on a plain client. These optional client mods give it better data, and it uses each one it finds. Nobody without them loses a feature. 🧠

🚀 nampower OctoLauncher mod

  • Your queued spell shows at the end of your cast bar
  • Spell casts and GUIDs for combat text and meters

How to get it: open OctoLauncher, go to its list of mods and tick nampower.

📏 UnitXP_SP3 OctoLauncher mod

  • Exact distances for range fading on nameplates and unit frames
  • Line of sight: a unit behind a wall counts as out of range

How to get it: in OctoLauncher's list of mods, tick unitXp.

🦸 SuperWoW by hand

  • Focus frame by GUID and /pfcast mouseover casting without switching target
  • Enemy cast bars on nameplates and target frames
  • Swing timer and meters read real casts, druid mana bar in forms

How to get it: download it from SuperWoW's releases, put the DLL next to WoW.exe and add its file name on its own line in dlls.txt in the same folder.

🧬 ClassicAPI by hand

  • Instant gear swaps and instant bag sorting
  • A real focus unit, cast bars that know "can't be interrupted"
  • The combat log for meters: exact crits, who interrupted what, dispels, kills
  • Map reveal for OctoWoW's own zones, item counts in tooltips, loot roll history, faster unit frames

How to get it: download it from ClassicAPI's releases, put ClassicAPI.dll next to WoW.exe and add ClassicAPI.dll on its own line in dlls.txt.

Start the game from OctoLauncher afterwards. OctoLauncher keeps the lines you add to dlls.txt. Close the game before you add a DLL.

🛠️ How to install

It takes about two minutes. ⏱️

  1. Close the game 🚪

    Addons are only read when the game starts.

  2. Put pfUI Octo in your AddOns folder 📁

    Download pfUI.zip, right-click it and choose Extract All, then Extract. Move the pfUI folder inside it into your game's AddOns folder, so it ends up like this: OctoWoW\Interface\AddOns\pfUI\pfUI.toc

    Already have pfUI? Replace the old pfUI folder: your settings are kept. The folder must be called pfUI (other addons look for that name).

  3. Play! 🎮

    Start the game from OctoLauncher. The first time, a short setup wizard helps you pick a look and a layout. After that:

    • /pfui opens the options window
    • /moveui opens layout mode to move and size frames
    • Optional: add the client mods above for even more

⌨️ Commands

/pfuiOpen the options window
/moveuiLayout mode: move, size, snap and hide frames
/layout [name]Switch to your next UI layout, or to the one you name
/gearGear sets: /gear naked, /gear queue, /gear specs
/keymapKey Map: a keyboard picture to drag your spells onto keys
/sctCombat text pages
/loothistoryWho rolled what on group loot (ClassicAPI)
/export, /importShare your whole setup as one text
/pfui installRun the setup wizard again
